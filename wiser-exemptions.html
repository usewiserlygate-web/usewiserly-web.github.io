Verification record

Rule

Identify the exact current exemption, exclusion, transition, or special-handling provision and authoritative URL.

Facts

Document which required facts are present, who validated them, and which source record supports each fact.

Time

Record effective and expiration dates, case/service date, source retrieval date, and any revalidation requirement.

Guardrails

  • Do not treat a participant conversation as a durable exemption unless official instructions support that status.
  • Do not reuse an exemption from another location, provider, supplier, beneficiary, code, or date.
  • Do not label an uncertain case “exempt”; escalate for official clarification.
  • Keep exemption status separate from ultimate coverage and payment requirements.
