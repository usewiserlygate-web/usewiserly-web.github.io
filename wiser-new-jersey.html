CMS WISeR in New Jersey

Build a repeatable intake-to-notice process while keeping official CMS and participant instructions authoritative.

Reviewed July 24, 2026 • CMS guide v7.0, updated July 24, 2026

WISeRly is independent, not affiliated with or endorsed by CMS, and is not an official WISeR participant. It does not issue determinations or guarantee authorization or payment. CMS and official participant instructions control.

New Jersey operating checklist

Intake

Capture the furnishing location, beneficiary program, anticipated date, service and code, setting, ordering information, and urgency claimed—then validate each item.

Route

Determine from current official materials whether prior authorization, pre-payment review, an exclusion, or other handling applies. Never infer a route from a similar case.

Close the loop

Retain the official confirmation and notice, record the actual official outcome, and route any next step to qualified staff.

Documentation quality controls

  • Include only records relevant to the selected service and current requirement.
  • Reconcile dates, laterality, service description, and identifiers across documents.
  • Do not manufacture missing evidence or overwrite the source record.
  • Track requests for additional information using the deadline in the official notice.
