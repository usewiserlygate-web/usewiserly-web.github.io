WISeR documentation readiness

Build a packet that is relevant, consistent, legible, and traceable to the source record—without manufacturing support.

Reviewed July 24, 2026 • CMS Provider and Supplier Guide v7.0

CMS requirements and assigned official participant instructions control. WISeRly is independent, not CMS-affiliated or endorsed, not an official WISeR participant, does not issue determinations, and does not guarantee authorization or payment.

Pre-submission checklist

Identity and service consistency

  • Correct beneficiary and authorized identifiers
  • Order, service, code, laterality, setting, and date align
  • Ordering and furnishing parties are clear

Clinical relevance

  • Records address current service-specific criteria
  • History, findings, tests, and treatment response are internally consistent
  • Time periods and sequence are understandable

Document integrity

  • Required signatures, credentials, and dates are present
  • Pages are readable and correctly ordered
  • Source documents and revisions remain traceable

Gap handling

  • Missing and contradictory facts are flagged
  • No facts are invented or silently changed
  • Qualified staff resolve issues through approved processes

Readiness is not a prediction

A packet can meet an internal completeness checklist and still receive non-affirmation or other official action. Conversely, a software warning does not establish ineligibility. Preserve this distinction in dashboards and conversations.

Human review: AI-assisted review can identify possible gaps, duplicates, or inconsistencies. It does not establish medical necessity and must not be represented as a human clinical determination. WISeRly provides no official result.
