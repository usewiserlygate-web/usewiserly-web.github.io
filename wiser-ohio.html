CMS WISeR in Ohio

Separate the scope decision, evidence review, official submission, and outcome so each can be checked.

Reviewed July 24, 2026 • CMS Provider and Supplier Guide v7.0

CMS publications and assigned official participant instructions control. WISeRly is independent, not CMS-affiliated or endorsed, not an official participant, does not issue determinations, and does not guarantee authorization or payment.

Four Ohio control points

Scope before documents

Confirm geography, beneficiary program, service/code, setting, date, and exclusions before requesting a chart packet.

Evidence before transmission

Check relevance, internal consistency, signatures, dates, legibility, and service-specific support without adding unsupported facts.

Channel before status

Use the assigned official participant’s current channel and retain its submission receipt. WISeRly cannot submit or check official status.

Notice before action

Read the exact official notice and deadline. Do not translate a preparation label into an affirmation or payment prediction.

Technology and clinical accountability

CMS discusses enhanced technology in the WISeR model. Technology or AI assistance may help prioritize or analyze material; human clinical determinations remain distinct. WISeRly’s synthetic preparation output is not either an official technology result or a determination.
