Step 2: Run Preparation Checks

Review applicability and documentation results, citations, rule versions, and any uncertainty requiring human action.

Step 3: Verify and Handoff

A qualified user verifies the package, then uses the assigned participant's official channel. WISeRly does not submit or determine the case.
