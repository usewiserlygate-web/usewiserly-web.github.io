Self-Paced Training Modules

Proposed curriculum; confirm current course availability and delivery details before purchase

Module 1: Understanding Healthcare Denial Patterns (60 minutes)

Learning Outcomes:
  • Identify the 5 most common denial triggers across VA, Medicaid, and commercial payers
  • Identify organization-specific inputs used to estimate administrative impact
  • Recognize the difference between hard denials vs soft denials
  • Understand that no denial reduction or authorization outcome is guaranteed
Key Topics:
  • How to evaluate cited external denial data without treating it as a WISeRly result
  • Differences between preparation software and official review channels
  • VA-specific: 38 CFR § 3.304(b) "Nexus" requirements
  • Pediatric-specific: EPSDT mandatory milestone documentation
  • Synthetic examples illustrating product limitations
Proposed materials; confirm included content before purchase

Module 2: WISeRly Platform Deep Dive (90 minutes)

Learning Outcomes:
  • Navigate the WISeRly workflows included in the current course offering
  • Configure specialty-specific rules and workflows
  • Interpret AI compliance scores (Red/Yellow/Green system)
  • Review preparation suggestions without treating them as clinical or official instructions
Interactive Activities:
  • Video demos: Review synthetic preparation examples
  • Practice exercises: Review synthetic preparation flags and next steps
  • Simulations: Set up your specialty's compliance rules
  • Dashboard walkthrough: Understand operational metrics and their limitations
Synthetic practice examples only; confirm availability

Module 3: Implementation & Integration (45 minutes)

Learning Outcomes:
  • Plan implementation activities without a guaranteed timeline
  • Identify any integration needs for separate technical scoping
  • Train staff on point-of-care workflows
  • Establish compliance monitoring protocols
Key Topics:
  • Pre-Implementation Checklist
  • Implementation planning topics
  • Go-live planning topics
  • Post-Launch Best Practices
Proposed templates and diagrams; confirm included content

Module 4: Advanced Use Cases & Knowledge Review (45 minutes estimated)

Learning Outcomes:
  • Apply WISeRly to complex multi-payer scenarios
  • Troubleshoot edge cases and unusual clinical situations
  • Review product knowledge through a course assessment if available
  • Understand that course completion is not an industry or government credential
Proposed Knowledge Review (confirm availability):
  • 20 multiple-choice questions
  • Must score 80% to pass (16/20 correct)
  • Unlimited retakes included
  • No professional certification or CEU is conferred
