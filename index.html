Preparation Workflow

Explainable Case Preparation

Use structured case information and cited rules to prepare a package for review outside WISeRly.

🎖️

Applicability Preparation

Review state, service, code, date, beneficiary program, place of service, and exclusions against the rule version shown.

👶

Documentation Checklist

Identify missing, conflicting, stale, or unsupported demonstration evidence with source citations.

🔗

Rule Provenance

See source URLs, publication and effective dates, retrieval dates, and rule-version freshness warnings.

📜

Activity History

Record case and workflow actions in the application activity history. No claim of regulatory certification is made.

📈

Human Review

Route ambiguous preparation results for assignment, notes, escalation, and a recorded human decision.

🔒

Account Controls

Account roles and server-side authorization support access control; real PHI remains blocked by a separate release gate.

📝

PHI Release Gate

Clinical submission remains disabled until required agreements, routing evidence, safeguards, and authorized activation are recorded. Signing up does not execute a BAA.

🎯

Participant-Ready Handoff

Prepare an export and track the reference and outcome entered from an official channel. WISeRly does not submit to participant portals or issue determinations.

Transparent Results

Preparation, Not Determination

Preparation results show the applicable rule, evidence found or missing, source version, confidence, and recommended next action.

🧠

Deterministic Rules First

Rule checks remain distinguishable from optional evidence suggestions.

📊

Source Citations

Users can inspect the source and version behind a result.

🔄

Human Judgment

Qualified users must verify results and use official participant channels.
